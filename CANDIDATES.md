## Candidates

1. **Routergeist** (24/25) — Router + Geist, the German word for spirit: a ghost living inside the router.
2. **Wifizard** (20/25) — Wi-Fi + Wizard: the network is run by a wizard casting connection spells.
3. **Modemghost** (18/25) — Modem + Ghost: a friendly ghost haunting the modem.
4. **Pingpolter** (18/25) — Ping + Poltergeist: a noisy spirit that rattles the connection.
5. **Bandwidthit** (17/25) — Bandwidth + Bandit: a bandit stealing your bandwidth.
6. **Netgoblin** (17/25) — Net + Goblin: a small goblin guarding the network.
7. **Routersaurus** (16/25) — Router + -saurus: an ancient dinosaur of a router.
8. **Signalspook** (16/25) — Signal + Spook: a spooky signal floating through the house.

## Winner

**Routergeist** — highest score (24/25).
